# Student Guide to AI Features

## Welcome to Your AI-Powered Academic Support System

This guide will help you understand and use the AI features designed to support your academic success.

## 🎯 What the AI Does for You

The AI analyzes your academic data to:
- Explain why your risk score changes
- Generate personalized advice for improvement
- Suggest achievable weekly goals
- Show you "what-if" scenarios (e.g., "What happens if I improve attendance?")

## 📊 Understanding Your Risk Score

### Why Did My Risk Change?
Navigate to **Student Portal → Risk Explanation**

You'll see:
- **Timeline**: Visual chart showing when your risk score changed
- **Explanation**: Plain-language narrative explaining the main drivers
- **Sources**: What data was used (attendance, grades, assignments)
- **Confidence Badge**: How certain the AI is (High/Medium/Low)

**Example**:
> "Your risk score increased by 12 points this week primarily due to **3 missed classes** in Statistics (Oct 2-6). The good news: your assignment scores remain strong at 87%. If you attend all classes next week, your score could improve by ~8 points."

### What the Icons Mean
- 🟢 **High Confidence**: Based on strong, complete data
- 🟡 **Medium Confidence**: Some uncertainty, but reasonable inference
- ⚪ **Low Confidence**: Limited data, treat as exploratory

## 💡 Getting Personalized Advice

### Ask the AI
Navigate to **Student Portal → AI Advisor**

**You can ask questions like**:
- "How can I improve my grades before finals?"
- "What should I focus on this week?"
- "Why is my attendance hurting my score?"

**The AI will**:
- Give you specific, actionable advice
- Explain its reasoning
- Cite the data it used
- Suggest 2-3 micro-goals

## 🔮 What-If Simulator

### Test Future Scenarios
Navigate to **Student Portal → What-If Simulator**

**How it works**:
1. Adjust sliders for attendance, grades, or assignments
2. AI predicts your new risk score
3. See explanations for each outcome

**Example**:
> "If you improve attendance from 70% to 90% over the next 4 weeks, your risk score could drop by approximately 15 points. This is because attendance is your #1 risk factor."

## 📅 Weekly Progress Brief

Every Monday, get an AI-generated summary:
- How you did last week
- What to focus on this week
- 2-3 achievable goals
- Celebrations of progress

## ❓ Understanding AI Explanations

### "Why Am I Seeing This?"
Click the ❓ icon on any AI suggestion to see:
- **Why this matters to you**
- **What data was used**
- **How the AI calculated this**
- **Your privacy protections**

### Source Citations
Look for italicized text like:
> *Based on attendance records from Oct 1-15*

This tells you where the AI got its information.

## 🔒 Your Privacy

### What You Should Know
- The AI only sees **your own data**
- You cannot see other students' information
- All AI interactions are logged for quality improvement
- Your data is never shared with sales teams

### Data the AI Uses
- Your attendance records
- Assignment submissions
- Grade data
- Previous interventions (if any)

The AI **cannot** see:
- Other students' data
- Financial information
- Medical records
- Personal communications

## ✅ Best Practices

### Do's
✅ Use AI advice as a **starting point**, not the final answer  
✅ Check the **confidence level** before acting  
✅ Ask questions if you don't understand  
✅ Celebrate small wins suggested by the AI  

### Don'ts
❌ Ignore advice just because it's AI-generated  
❌ Assume low-confidence suggestions are wrong  
❌ Share your login with others  
❌ Rely solely on AI - talk to your counselor too  

## 🆘 When to Seek Human Help

The AI is great, but it's not a replacement for:
- **Mental health support**: Contact campus counseling
- **Financial aid questions**: Talk to financial aid office
- **Medical issues**: See healthcare professionals
- **Personal crises**: Reach out to a counselor or advisor

**If you see a ⚠️ warning**, it means the AI detected a sensitive topic and recommends speaking with a human advisor.

## 🌟 Tips for Success

1. **Check your dashboard weekly**: Stay on top of changes
2. **Try the What-If Simulator**: Explore different strategies
3. **Set micro-goals**: AI suggests small, achievable steps
4. **Read the "Why"**: Understanding helps you learn patterns
5. **Combine AI + Human Advice**: Use both for best results

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the AI always right?**  
A: No. The AI makes predictions based on patterns in data. Always check the confidence level and use your judgment.

**Q: Can I turn off AI features?**  
A: Yes. Navigate to Settings → Privacy and toggle "AI Advisor" off.

**Q: Does the AI replace my academic advisor?**  
A: No. The AI complements human advisors by surfacing insights they might not see in large datasets.

**Q: What happens if I disagree with the AI?**  
A: That's okay! You can ignore suggestions and talk to a human advisor. Your feedback helps improve the system.

**Q: How often does the AI update?**  
A: Risk explanations update in real-time as new data comes in (usually daily).
